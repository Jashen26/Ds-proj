K.W.A.R.T.A.

Rules and Regulation

I. Eligibility Requirements

  1. Applicants must be at least eighteen (18) years old or have a parent/legal guardian apply on their behalf.
  2. Applicants must provide complete, accurate, and truthful information.
  3. Applicants must submit all required supporting documents.
  4. Priority shall be given to financially disadvantaged individuals and families.
  5. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval of assistance.

II. Application Rules

  1. Only one active application per household shall be allowed at any given time.
  2. Duplicate applications may result in automatic disqualification.
  3. Incomplete applications shall not be processed until all requirements are submitted.
  4. Falsified documents or misleading information shall result in rejection of the application.
  5. K.W.A.R.T.A. reserves the right to verify all information and documents submitted.

III. Required Documents

Mandatory Documents:

  • Valid Government-Issued ID
  • Barangay Certificate
  • Recent Photo / Selfie

IV. Verification Process

  1. All submitted documents shall undergo verification.
  2. Applicants may be required to attend interviews or provide additional supporting documents.
  3. Home visits may be conducted when necessary.
  4. Applications shall only proceed to evaluation after successful verification.

V. Approval and Assistance

  1. Assistance shall be granted based on eligibility, verified need, and available funds.
  2. The amount of assistance shall be determined by the evaluation committee.
  3. Approved applicants may receive full or partial assistance.
  4. Decisions made by the approval committee shall be considered final.

VI. Beneficiary Responsibilities

  1. Beneficiaries shall use the assistance solely for its intended purpose.
  2. Beneficiaries may be required to provide proof of fund utilization.
  3. Beneficiaries shall cooperate during monitoring and evaluation activities.
  4. Misuse of assistance may affect future eligibility.

VII. Program Duration and Exit Policy

A. Program Participation Period

  1. Beneficiaries may remain enrolled in the K.W.A.R.T.A. Program for a maximum period of seven (7) years from the date of approval.
  2. The program aims to help beneficiaries achieve financial stability and self-sufficiency.

B. Semi-Annual Income Verification

  1. All active beneficiaries must submit updated proof of income every six (6) months.
  2. Acceptable proof of income may include:
    • Certificate of Employment
    • Latest Payslip
    • Certificate of Unemployment
    • Business Income Statement
    • Social Worker Assessment
    • Other valid proof of income required by the program

C. Beneficiary Monitoring

  1. Beneficiaries shall undergo financial reassessment every six (6) months.
  2. The reassessment shall determine whether the beneficiary remains qualified for assistance.
  3. Failure to comply with reassessment requirements may result in suspension of benefits.

D. Graduation from the Program

A beneficiary may be considered for graduation when:

  • The seven-year participation period has been completed;
  • The beneficiary demonstrates financial stability;
  • The beneficiary secures stable employment or a sustainable livelihood;
  • The beneficiary voluntarily withdraws from the program.

E. Self-Sufficiency Requirement

  1. K.W.A.R.T.A. is designed to provide temporary assistance while encouraging beneficiaries to achieve financial independence.
  2. Beneficiaries who are physically and legally able to work shall actively pursue employment, livelihood opportunities, skills training, or other income-generating activities.
  3. Every three (3) months, beneficiaries shall submit proof of self-sufficiency efforts, which may include:
    • Certificate of Employment
    • Recent Payslips
    • Proof of Job Applications
    • Skills Training Certificates
    • Livelihood or Small Business Records
    • Participation in Community Employment Programs
    • Other supporting documents approved by the program
  4. Beneficiaries who are unable to work due to age, disability, serious medical conditions, or caregiving responsibilities may be exempted upon submission of appropriate supporting documents.
  5. Failure to provide proof of self-sufficiency efforts for two (2) consecutive review periods without valid justification may result in suspension, reassessment, or removal from the program.

F. Program Exit

A beneficiary may be removed from the program if:

  • False information or fraudulent documents are submitted;
  • Assistance funds are misused;
  • Required six-month income verification documents are not submitted;
  • Required three-month self-sufficiency reports are not submitted;
  • Program rules are repeatedly violated;
  • The beneficiary no longer meets eligibility requirements.

VIII. Fraud Prevention

  1. Providing false information is strictly prohibited.
  2. Submission of fake or altered documents shall result in immediate disqualification.
  3. Attempts to manipulate the system or obtain multiple benefits unlawfully shall result in permanent disqualification.
  4. Suspected fraudulent activities may be investigated by program administrators.

IX. Data Privacy and Confidentiality

  1. Personal information shall be collected and processed in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012.
  2. Information shall be used solely for verification, evaluation, monitoring, and program administration.
  3. Access to personal data shall be restricted to authorized personnel only.
  4. Beneficiary information shall not be disclosed without proper authorization unless required by law.

X. Administrator Code of Conduct

  1. Administrators shall treat all applicants fairly and without discrimination.
  2. Confidential information shall be protected at all times.
  3. Administrators shall avoid conflicts of interest.
  4. All approvals, rejections, and fund releases must be properly documented.
  5. Abuse of authority may result in disciplinary action, suspension, or removal from the program.

XI. Beneficiary Commitment

By participating in the K.W.A.R.T.A. Program, beneficiaries agree to:

  1. Provide truthful and accurate information.
  2. Submit updated proof of income every six (6) months.
  3. Submit proof of self-sufficiency efforts every three (3) months.
  4. Cooperate with monitoring and verification activities.
  5. Use assistance only for its intended purpose.
  6. Actively seek employment, livelihood opportunities, skills training, or other means of improving financial stability.
  7. Work toward financial independence and self-sufficiency.

XII. Grounds for Disqualification

Applicants or beneficiaries may be disqualified for:

  • Submission of fake or falsified documents;
  • Multiple applications under different identities;
  • Misrepresentation of financial status;
  • Misuse of assistance funds;
  • Failure to submit required reports and documents;
  • Repeated violations of program rules and regulations.
