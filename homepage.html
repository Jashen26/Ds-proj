About K.W.A.R.T.A.

Bridging the gap
for every Filipino

Kaagapay ng Walang-wala: Ayuda, Relief, at Tulong sa Araw-araw

We are a dedicated financial assistance program designed to uplift underprivileged Filipino citizens who face daily struggles in affording basic necessities. Our mission is to bridge economic gaps by providing essential aid for food, education, healthcare, and household expenses.

Guided by a targeted assessment system, we evaluate applicant needs using key socioeconomic variables—such as income, source of livelihood, family size, and bills—to ensure our support goes directly to the individuals and families who need it most. Together, we strive to build a more supportive and resilient community.

We deliver dependable financial solutions tailored for those navigating critical needs.
Our specialized support includes:

FOOD
EDUCATION
HEALTH CARE
HOUSE HOLD BILLS
TRANSPORTATION
FUNERAL AID
