Before You Apply

What to Know Before You Apply

KWARTA gives monthly financial assistance to Filipino citizens who need it most. Read through the eligibility rules, assistance tiers, and application steps below before you start your application — once submitted, it can't be edited.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility Criteria

Every applicant must meet all three base requirements, plus belong to at least one category below.

Filipino Citizen

You must hold Filipino citizenship.

PH Resident

You must currently reside in the Philippines.

Valid ID

A current, unexpired government-issued ID is required.

plus one of these categories

Low Income Household

No additional ID required beyond your valid government ID.

Solo Parent

Must provide a valid Solo Parent ID.

PWD

Must provide a valid PWD ID.

How Much You Could Receive

Assistance Tiers

Applications are scored on a 14-point scale. Your final score determines which tier you're placed in.

Tier Points Score Range Monthly Amount Who It's For
Full Assistance 10–14 ₱10,000 Highest financial vulnerability or urgent need; meets or exceeds all core criteria.
High Assistance 7–9 ₱7,500 Significant financial hardship, but doesn't meet the maximum criteria.
Moderate Assistance 4–6 ₱5,000 Mid-level financial strain with some stable resources, still needs steady support.
Limited Assistance 1–3 ₱2,500 Minor financial gap; acts as a safety net for small hardships.
Not Eligible 0 — Did not meet the minimum threshold for assistance.

Heads up: your source guidelines list "Not Eligible" as requiring 7–9 points, which is the same range as High Assistance. I've shown it as 0 points / below minimum threshold instead — double check the intended cutoff before you publish this.

Keep Your Application Valid

Application Guidelines

Do

  • Provide a valid, secure email address
  • Provide a working phone number
  • Apply with a stable internet connection
  • Upload clear and legible documents

Don't

  • Submit expired IDs
  • Falsify information
  • Submit multiple applications
  • Share your account password with others
  • Pay anyone to apply on your behalf

Track Your Application

Application Status Guide

Review typically takes 3–5 business days. Here's what each status means.

Pending Review

Your application has been submitted successfully and is currently in queue.

Under Review

An administrator is currently evaluating your application.

Approved

Your application passed evaluation. You'll start receiving assistance based on your tier.

Declined

You didn't meet the eligibility criteria — either a document couldn't be verified, or your application scored zero points.

Three Steps

How to Apply

  1. 01

    Register

    Visit KWARTA to create your account.

  2. 02

    Fill Out the Forms

    Complete each section and upload your supporting documents:

    • Personal Information
    • Household Information
    • Economic Information
    • Supporting Documents
  3. 03

    Review and Submit

    Double-check everything before submitting. Once submitted, your application can no longer be edited.
