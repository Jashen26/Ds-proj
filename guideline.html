Guidelines

KWARTA gives monthly financial assistance to Filipino citizens who need it most. Read through the eligibility rules, assistance levels, and application steps below before starting your application.

Eligibility Criteria

Every applicant must meet all three base requirements, plus belong to at least one category below.

🇵🇭

Filipino Citizen

You must hold Filipino citizenship.

📍

PH Resident

You must currently reside in the Philippines.

🪪

Valid Government ID

A current, unexpired government-issued ID is required.

Additional categories
💰

Low Income Household

No additional ID required beyond your valid government ID.

🤝

Solo Parent

Must provide a valid Solo Parent ID.

♿

Person with Disability (PWD)

Must provide a valid PWD ID.

Assistance Levels

Applications are scored on a 14-point scale. Your final score determines which tier you are placed in.

Tier Points Score Range Monthly Amount Who It's For
✅ Full Assistance 10–14 ₱10,000 Highest financial vulnerability; meets or exceeds all core criteria.
✅ High Assistance 7–9 ₱7,500 Significant financial hardship, but doesn't meet the maximum criteria.
✅ Moderate Assistance 4–6 ₱5,000 Mid-level financial strain with some stable resources.
✅ Limited Assistance 1–3 ₱2,500 Minor financial gap; acts as a safety net for small hardships.
❌ Not Eligible 0 ₱0 Did not meet the minimum threshold for assistance.
Keep Your Application Valid

Application Guidelines

Do
  • Provide a valid, secure email address
  • Provide a working phone number
  • Apply with a stable internet connection
  • Upload clear and legible documents
Don't
  • Submit expired documents
  • Falsify any information
  • Submit multiple applications
  • Share your account password with others

Application Status

Review typically takes 3–5 business days. Here's what each status means.

Under Review

An administrator is evaluating your application and supporting documents.

Approved

Your application passed evaluation. You'll start receiving assistance based on your tier.

Rejected

You didn't meet the eligibility criteria or your documents could not be verified.

How to Apply

  1. 1

    Register

    Create your account on the portal using a valid email address and a secure password.

  2. 2

    Fill Out the Forms

    Complete each section and upload your supporting documents:

    • Applicant Information
    • Household Information
    • Economic Status
  3. 3

    Review and Submit

    Double-check everything before submitting. Once submitted, your application can no longer be edited.
